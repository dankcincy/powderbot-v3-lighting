# March report evidence for the v1_13 lighting baseline

Sources are original DOCX files under [project-drive]/Desktop/ESP32/Powder Thrower/Project Reports, unless otherwise indicated. Adjacent TXT extracts preserve document order with P = body block ordinal and T = table/row ordinal. These are extraction locators, not Word page numbers.

## Observed in original evidence

- March 21, autotrickler_v3_comm_report.docx, section 1 Proven system view, T03.02–.05, and Appendix P059–P066. The three embedded photos are byte-identical to Pics/Pin11 Auto.JPG, Pics/Pin12 Active in Auto.JPG, and Pics/Target Weight Confirm.JPG. Original module TX carries app-to-V3 data. ST232C 11 is the V3-command logic path and 12 is scale-data logic path. Section 3 T06.04 reports the nearby left-edge pads as RS-232 side. Section 6 P049–P053 says BLE 9600 is observed, scale rate is separate, ST232C logic is apparently 5 V and the edge pads carry RS-232 levels.
- March 22, PowderBot_9000_Implementation_Report_v0_1.docx, section 2 T02.02–.05 and section 3 T03.02–.07. Working board is specifically JC2432W328C ESP32 touchscreen. MAX3232 R1OUT -> GPIO35 was confirmed by readable scale data, R2OUT -> GPIO22 by command chatter, and BLE module TX -> GPIO21 by readable app intent. Onboard LED reported active-low 4/R, 16/G, 17/B. This is the first onboard-light prototype.
- March 22 original board photos in Pics: IMG_20260322_174951805_HDR.jpg shows P6 labeled GND/IO16/IO4/IO17; 174957884 shows middle CN1 GND/IO22/IO21/3V3; 175001579 shows P3 silkscreen GND/IO35/IO22/IO21 next to the TF slot. 175054577 shows the separate connector markings +5V/TXD/RXD/GND. The photos have no plugs installed and do not identify the later harness colors or which overlapping GPIO21/22 connector was used. Board..JPG is an Arduino IDE selector screenshot from a Waveshare profile exploration, not physical model evidence.
- March 22, expanded v1.2, autotrickler_v3_expanded_summary_report_with_screenshots_v1_2.docx, T01.05, T04.03–.05, P026, Figure E1 P119–P120. CC2541 chip marking is legible in front-side Figure E1. Carrier 3.3 V logic is described as apparent, and 19200 scale baud as a working assumption. Separate original rear photo Pics/IMG_20260317_160614693_HDR~2.jpg has ZS-040 carrier silkscreen LEVEL:3.3V, Power:3.6–6V and STATE/RXD/TXD/GND/VCC/EN labels (parent visual check). The marked carrier supply range does not identify the actual assembled V3 rail.
- March 23, PowderBot_9000_Implementation_Report_v0_2.docx T03, sections 2 and 7 P013–P020, P043–P050. Strip bench wiring is GPIO17 -> 330 ohm data series resistor -> DI, external 5 V strip supply and ESP32 common ground, 22 pixels, tested segment top/middle/bottom pads GND/data/+5V as described. 470 uF was removed after a power-kill-like result. GPIO17 doubles as onboard LED connection. Traffic timing inferred BLE connect/disconnect in this early build, and GPIO22 was still documented as scale chatter.
- March 23, autotrickler_v3_expanded_summary_report_with_screenshots_v1_3.docx, P011–P014, P022, P037, confirms same strip bring-up, notes GPIO4 flicker motivated GPIO17, and records preference for brightness 100. Its active sketch is the earlier named personality V1 baseline. The 22-pixel wiring carries into v1_13 per source.
- March 24–25 Master v1.0–v1.3 retain March 22 hardware proof and March 23 strip baseline. v1.0 section 8 P059–P065, T13–T15 is a clear example showing GPIO22 command chatter in those older milestones.
- March 26, PowderBot_9000_Master_Project_Report_v1_4.docx T07.03 and v1_5 Appendix E show real observed BLE connect/disconnect on GPIO22 by v1_8. v1_6 P010 and Appendix E T11.02–.04, Appendix F T12.02–.10 and Appendix H T13.06/P085 carry this into v1_13 and explicitly list v1_13 as bench-verified. Original V3 dependency on STATE in a replacement system and independent live scale baud confirmation remained open. GPIO22 is no longer the MAX3232 R2OUT tap in this baseline.
- March 26, PowderBot_9000_Comprehensive_Master_Project_Report_v2_1.docx P002 and T01.03. The file name says v2_1, the internal title says v2.0, and it separates the last bench-validated v1_13 from current renovation on powderbot_core_v2. Chronology T06.02/.05/.08/.10 and electrical table T08 support the scale path, 5 V ST232C concern and 19200 assumption. P062/T10 distinguish validated visual STATE input from original V3 dependency. Appendix B embeds BLE software screenshots rather than detailed original harness photos.
- v1_13 sketch source, Firmware/Arduino/Sketchbook/powderbot_personality_v1_13/powderbot_personality_v1_13.ino lines 3, 37–39, 57–82, 125, 1430–1433, 1443: JC2432W328C (Arduino ESP32 Dev Module); module TX -> GPIO21, BLE STATE -> GPIO22 active high, R1OUT -> GPIO35, strip -> GPIO17 through 330 ohms, external 5 V strip common GND, 22 pixels NEO_GRB/800 kHz; BLE 9600 and scale 19200 8N1. Both taps are receive-only in source. The compiled baud and bench-verified light behavior strongly support working scale decoding at that rate, while the March report did not close independent wire-rate verification.
- April 1 BLE Takeover Bringup v0_1/v0_2 section 3 P015–P016 and T02.02 confirm silkscreen is module-relative: TXD drives V3 RX with app intent, RXD receives the V3 reply. That physical-direction finding corroborates the March passive tap; replacement hardware is a later configuration.
- April 12 file timestamp: Firmware/Arduino/Sketchbook/Command Testing/Bart/scale_comms_v0_2/FX-120i_123 Quick Start Guide.docx section 1 T02 and section 2 T03 report separately bench-validated FX-123 setup RESPONSE FAST, GN, SiF bPS 5/19200, btPr 2/8N1, bASFnc SPd 2/20 Hz, host CRLF. Host TX22/RX33 is from that later direct-connection experiment. The archived guide cannot establish March V3 menu settings or March wire colors, and its FX-i command-family scope is explicit.

## Inferred or open

- CORRECTION after inspecting recovered original photos: IMG_20260322_172007367_HDR.jpg establishes blue on molded 2, green on 3, black on 5; IMG_20260322_171847532_HDR~2.jpg and 174154299 show red on molded 9. The previous red-on-5 reading was incorrect. These are rear-view contact numbers, not independently established signal/power functions. Exact numbered-contact-to-MAX3232-R1IN continuity remains unproven.
- The tested March translating breakout is identified as MAX3232 and the logic-facing R1OUT connection is reported. The inspected March reports do not document its exact BOM/model, VCC feed and wire color, its RS-232 input landing, or exact final ESP32 power source. The later Bart 3.3 V recommendation/module wiring is a distinct circuit.
- The March reports establish a working connect/disconnect input; they do not close where the V3 used STATE internally, or record complete final harness photos. The original carrier header and sketch together support the STATE tap reconstruction.
- No same-era source reviewed gives full FX-123 menu values, power supply current rating, or exact strip part number. The original bench reports state electrical function and behavior but do not establish today's hardware state.
