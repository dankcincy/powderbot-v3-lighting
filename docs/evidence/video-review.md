## Documentary cross-check and unresolved items

- March 22 Implementation Report v0.1 identifies the original yellow ESP32 display as JC2432W328C. The March 23 and March 26 videos show the yellow display board face but lack legible reverse-side model/revision or connector marks, so the model association comes from the contemporaneous report and project board photos. The dark CrowPanel clip documents separate hardware.
- March 23 Implementation Report v0.2 and March 26 v1_13 sketch document GPIO17 through 330 ohms to strip DI, external 5 V for the 22-pixel strip, and common ground. These are documentary observations, not endpoint confirmations from the movies. The black-covered section in the yellow lead's path cannot be identified visually as the resistor. Colors do not establish electrical roles.
- The clips do not show strip pad labels, ESP32 GPIO pad contacts, a readable model/revision for the small breadboard PCB or white-mounted assembly, a traceable power-feed endpoint, actual strip voltage, source rating, regulator/buck identity, common-ground tie point, or USB cable source. The visible loose barrel plug is unmated during operation and cannot identify how the working ring was powered.
