# powderbot_merge_v0_3 notes

This is the first real merge skeleton, not the final merged machine.

What it does now:
- Keeps the known-good `powderbot_comms_v1_2` bridge behavior and Bart's-board pin map.
- Adds comms callbacks so BLE connect/disconnect, parsed app commands, and parsed scale samples can drive core logic.
- Keeps `powderbot_engine`, `powderbot_presentation`, and `powderbot_strip_renderer` as separate donor modules.
- Uses a thin `powderbot_merge_adapter` seam to keep transport ownership out of the engine.

What it intentionally does **not** do yet:
- It does not move machine-control replies/status generation into the adapter.
- It does not take ownership of run_throw / run_trickler / stop_trickler / throw_on / throw_off.
- It does not replace the V3 bridge. App traffic is still forwarded through the comms hub.

Architectural rule for later:
- Comms stays portable enough to move to the display board.
- Core stays transport-blind.
- The adapter stays thin and disposable.
